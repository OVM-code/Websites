How this works — and why it's different

I don't show up with a bid and a deadline. We start by working together inside your business, as a paid engagement with clear scope. You see how I operate; I learn how your business really runs. Only then — if it fits for both of us — do we talk about an option to buy, on terms we shape together.

  1. A quiet conversation

    We meet. You tell me about the business and what you want for it and for yourself. Strictly confidential — no teasers, no lists, no leaks.

  2. Working inside the business

    A paid, scoped engagement where I take real work off your plate and prove value before anything about ownership is on the table.

  3. An option, not an ultimatum

    If we both want it, we agree an option to buy. You keep control until you choose to hand it over — and you'll know exactly who is taking care of it.

Buy to fix and keep — never strip and flip. The plan is always to run and grow the business with the people in it. Leanness comes from modern back-office systems at the holding level, not from cutting the people who do the actual work.

This might fit if…

  • Your business is fundamentally sound but has been coasting — you know there's more in it than it's showing.
  • There's no natural successor, and the usual buyers feel wrong for what you built.
  • You want continuity for your people and your customers, not just the best multiple.
  • You'd rather test the fit in practice than sign your name on faith.

No pitch. Just a conversation.

Write a few lines about your business and your situation. You'll get a thoughtful, confidential reply — and a clear "not a fit" if that's the honest answer.
