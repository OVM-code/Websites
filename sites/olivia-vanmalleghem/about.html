About

Hi, I'm Olivia.

I'm a Belgian builder and operator. The shortest honest summary: I like taking things that should work better — teams, businesses, my own head — and building the system that makes them work better.

What I actually do

Three things, and they feed each other. I speak and run workshops on putting AI to work in real organizations — not the demo, the Monday-morning reality. I build ventures and systems of my own, which keeps the advice honest: everything I recommend, I run somewhere myself. And through a small holding I acquire sound Belgian businesses from owners who are ready to step back — to keep and grow them, with a modern back-office and the people who made them work in the first place.

The common thread is leverage without theatre. I'm allergic to busywork, to slide decks that promise transformation, and to "AI strategies" that never touch an actual workflow. What I like: small teams, clear systems, honest numbers, and tools that quietly do their job.

I work in Dutch and English, from Belgium, with occasional strong opinions about both.
